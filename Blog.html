Topic: How to preserve your flowers?

If you have received a flower bouquet as a gift and want it to stay fresh for weeks, follow these tips:

1. Always keep the bouquet in a vase (pot) filled with clean water.
2. Cut the stems of the flowers slightly at a diagonal (angled) from the bottom.
3. Make sure to change the water every two days.
4. Keep the bouquet away from direct sunlight.
