Topic: Phoolon ko lambe samay tak fresh kaise rakhein?

Agar aapko koi flower bouquet gift mila hai aur aap chahte hain ki wo hafton tak fresh rahe, toh in tips ko follow karein:

1. Bouquet ko hamesha saaf paani ke vase (pot) mein rakhein.
2. Phoolon ki dandi (stem) ko niche se thoda tirchha (diagonal) kaatein.
3. Paani ko har do din mein zaroor badlein.
4. Bouquet ko sidha dhoop (direct sunlight) se door rakhein.
